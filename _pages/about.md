I am a Master of Science in Intelligent Information Systems (MIIS) student at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/) in the School of Computer Science (Aug 2026 – Dec 2027).

Previously, I was a Pre-doctoral Researcher at [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/) (May 2025 – Jul 2026), where I worked on active evaluation for foundation models, mechanistic interpretability in Vision-Language Models (VLMs), and cultural alignment for Gemini. Prior to that, I was a Research Assistant at the [Social AI Studio](https://www.socialai.studio/) at the [Singapore University of Technology and Design](https://sutd.edu.sg/) (SUTD) led by [Dr. Roy Ka-Wei Lee](https://info.roylee.sg/). I graduated from SUTD with a Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science (Minor in Artificial Intelligence), including a semester abroad at the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://umd.edu/) where I was awarded Semester Academic Honours.

### Research Interests
My research centers around the evaluation, interpretability, and alignment of Large Language Models and Vision-Language Models:
1. **Active AI Evaluation & Proactive Failure Discovery:** Developing Bayesian inference and transfer-learned Gaussian Process surrogate frameworks (such as [ProEval](https://icml.cc/)) to efficiently discover model failures and estimate performance across LLMs and VLMs.
2. **Mechanistic Interpretability & Cross-Modal Steering:** Identifying underlying neural circuits responsible for cross-lingual and multimodal gaps via activation patching, and applying representation steering across modalities.
3. **Multilingual & Cultural Alignment & Safety:** Designing reference-guided critique reward modeling for RLHF, agentic information-gain evaluation, and robust safety/functional benchmarks for low-resource languages and multimodal media (e.g., [SEAHateCheck](https://dl.acm.org/) and [MATK](https://dl.acm.org/)).
