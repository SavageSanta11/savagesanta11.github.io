Research Interests

My research centers on the evaluation, interpretability, and alignment of Large Language Models (LLMs) and Vision-Language Models (VLMs):

  • Active AI Evaluation & Failure Discovery: Designing Bayesian inference and transfer-learned Gaussian Process surrogate frameworks (such as ProEval) to efficiently discover systematic model failures and estimate performance across LLMs and VLMs.
  • Mechanistic Interpretability & Model Steering: Identifying underlying neural circuits responsible for cross-lingual and cross-modal bottlenecks via activation patching, and applying representation steering across modalities.
  • Multilingual & Cultural Alignment & Safety: Developing critique reward modeling for RLHF, information-theoretic evaluation, and robust safety benchmarks for low-resource languages and multimodal media (e.g., SEAHateCheck and MATK).
